# Findings and Recommendations

# Hotel Booking Cancellation Analysis

---

# Executive Summary

This analysis examined 44,480 hotel booking records to identify the key drivers of reservation cancellations and their impact on business performance.

The study found that cancellation behavior is strongly influenced by room pricing, booking lead time, booking channels, and seasonal demand patterns.

Overall, 28.33% of all hotel bookings were cancelled, representing a significant source of lost revenue and operational inefficiency.

# Research Question 1

## What factors influence hotel reservation cancellations?

### Findings

The analysis identified four primary drivers of cancellations:

1. Room Pricing (ADR)
2. Lead Time
3. Booking Source
4. Seasonal Demand

### Conclusion

Booking cancellations are driven by both customer behavior and operational factors.

---

# Research Question 2

## How can cancellation rates be reduced?

### Findings

The following factors were associated with lower cancellation rates:

- Competitive room pricing
- Shorter booking lead times
- Direct booking channels
- Corporate customer segments

### Conclusion

Hotels should focus on pricing optimization, direct booking incentives, and customer retention programs.

---

# Research Question 3

## How can pricing and promotional strategies be improved?

### Findings

Cancelled reservations had significantly higher average room rates.

| Booking Status | Average ADR |
|----------------|------------|
| Not Cancelled | 90.55 |
| Cancelled | 104.83 |

### Insight

Customers appear more sensitive to higher room prices and may seek alternative accommodations when prices increase.

### Business Impact

Pricing strategy directly influences booking retention.

# Pricing Analysis

## ADR and Cancellation Behavior

| Booking Status | Average ADR |
|---------------|-------------|
| Not Cancelled | 90.55 |
| Cancelled | 104.83 |

### Finding

Cancelled bookings had higher room rates than completed bookings.

### Insight

Higher prices increase the likelihood of customer cancellations.

---

# Hypothesis Testing

## Hypothesis 1

### Statement

More cancellations occur when prices are higher.

### Result

| Status | Result |
|---------|---------|
| Hypothesis | Supported |

### Evidence

Cancelled bookings showed an average ADR of 104.83 compared to 90.55 for completed bookings.

### Conclusion

Higher room prices are associated with increased cancellation rates.

# Lead Time Analysis

| Lead Time Group | Bookings | Cancellations |
|----------------|----------|---------------|
| 0–30 Days | 17,143 | 2,390 |
| 31–90 Days | 10,559 | 3,531 |
| 91–180 Days | 8,998 | 3,451 |
| 180+ Days | 7,780 | 3,228 |

### Findings

Longer booking lead times generated substantially higher cancellation volumes.

### Insight

Customers who reserve further in advance have more opportunities to change travel plans.

### Business Impact

Advance reservations carry a greater cancellation risk.

---

# Waiting List Analysis

| Booking Status | Average Waiting Days |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Not Cancelled | 0.8297 |
| Cancelled | 0.1247 |

### Observation

The results did not fully support the original expectation that longer waiting periods increase cancellations.

### Conclusion

Further investigation may be required to understand the relationship between waiting-list duration and customer behavior.

# Hypothesis 2

### Statement

Customers with longer waiting-list periods cancel more frequently.

### Result

⚠️ Inconclusive

### Reason

Cancelled bookings actually showed fewer average waiting days than non-cancelled bookings.

# Recommendations

## Recommendation 1: Implement Dynamic Pricing

Use demand-based pricing models to maintain competitive room rates during peak periods.

### Expected Outcome

Reduced price-related cancellations.

---

## Recommendation 2: Encourage Direct Bookings

Offer exclusive discounts and loyalty rewards for direct reservations.

### Expected Outcome

Reduced OTA dependency.

---

## Recommendation 3: Strengthen Cancellation Policies

Introduce partial deposits or tiered cancellation fees for long lead-time reservations.

### Expected Outcome

Reduced cancellation risk.

---

## Recommendation 4: Improve Lead-Time Management

Offer incentives for customers to confirm reservations closer to arrival dates.

### Expected Outcome

More reliable occupancy forecasting.

---

## Recommendation 5: Focus on Peak Season Retention

Implement targeted customer engagement campaigns during high-demand months.

### Expected Outcome

Improved booking retention.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis revealed that booking cancellations are primarily driven by pricing, booking lead time, booking channels, and seasonal demand fluctuations.

By implementing targeted pricing strategies, reducing OTA dependence, and improving reservation policies, Lagoon Island Lounge and Resort Hotel can reduce cancellation rates, improve occupancy, and increase revenue performance.
